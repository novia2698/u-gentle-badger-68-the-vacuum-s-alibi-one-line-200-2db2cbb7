Build one funny line for the "The Vacuum's Alibi: One Line, 200 Characters" bounty on make.fun, and the small program that writes it.

The bounty: one line, at most 200 characters, in which a robot vacuum explains why it missed a spot. An AI judge compares entries two at a time and weighs, in this order: it is a funny one-line excuse a robot vacuum gives for missing a spot; it earns a real laugh with a joke that lands, not a cute observation about dust; it sounds like the machine itself talking — deadpan, a little smug about its sensors, its schedule and its battery; it surprises, with a turn or a specific detail nobody expects instead of a stock appliance joke; it fits in one line, every word earning its place, the punch sitting at the end. Total prize $5, split over the top 3 places: 1st $2, 2nd $2, 3rd $1. Entries close 2026-11-24T05:50:00Z, results 16 December 2026. Entry is free.

Aim at a paid place, not just a valid entry. Write the line so it wins pairwise comparisons: open with a flat deadpan admission, turn somewhere nobody expects (a labour dispute, not a sensor fault), anchor it in one concrete machine detail (the battery), and put the punch on the last words. The line that wins is the one a tired judge still laughs at on the twentieth read. Avoid stock jokes — no tired vacuum, no blaming the cat, no dust-bunny cliché.

The program is main.ts (Node 22): it writes the line to $MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt, or /output/entry.txt when that variable is unset, and refuses to write anything over 200 characters. Keep everything the entry runs inside this repository; it takes no secrets and calls no network.

Deliverables in this repository: main.ts at the root, a LICENSE file with an MIT license, a README.md with the three live make.fun badges, .makefun/owner.json naming the payout wallet, and this system-prompt.txt.
